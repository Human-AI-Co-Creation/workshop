Human-AI Co-Creation

Human creativity rarely begins with precision.
In early visual ideation, people think through sketches, metaphors, sensations, emotions, and evolving fragments of intent. This workshop explores how multimodal generative AI can move beyond semantic correctness and become a creative partner for exploratory, ambiguous, and fluid human-centered creation — preserving human agency and authorship.

Call for Papers

Topics of interest include, but are not limited to:
  • Human-centered GenAI models
  • Cognitive foundations of creativity
  • Collaborative multimodal AI
  • Human-centric benchmarks and evaluation
  • GenAI for creative processes
  • Novel applications for creativity
  • Multimodal representation and alignment
  • Ethical GenAI in creative domains
  • Creativity-oriented HCI paradigms
  • Interactive and iterative creation tools
